## 1. Who we are
Forge Code Academy ("Forge", "we", "us") is an online tool for learning JavaScript
and C#. It is run by [operator's full legal name or business name], [city], Nigeria.

Contact for anything in this policy, including data requests:
**forgecodeacademy.help@gmail.com**

This policy is written to meet the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 (NDPA) and
should be read with our Terms of Use.

## 2. What we collect

### If you use Forge without an account
Your learning data (completed lessons, quiz answers, code drafts, notes, review
schedules, project progress, focus time, preferences) is saved **only in your
browser**. It is not sent to us.

### If you create an account
- **Account details:** email address, a display name if you choose one, and a
  password. Passwords are stored only as a secure hash by our authentication
  provider; we never see or store the password itself.
- **Sign-in with Google or GitHub:** if you choose this, we receive your email
  address and basic profile name from that provider. We do not receive your
  password for those services.
- **Synced learning data:** the same progress record described above, so it can be
  backed up and shared across your devices.
- **Optional features:** a leaderboard name and XP if you choose to join the
  leaderboard; referral codes if you use or share one; team or school membership
  if you join a team.
- **Payments (when paid plans are live):** your plan, its status and renewal date.
  Card details are handled entirely by Paystack; we never see or store them.

### Collected automatically
- **Usage analytics:** if enabled, PostHog records pages visited and feature use
  to help us improve Forge. It is switched off when your browser sends a
  "Do Not Track" or "Global Privacy Control" signal.
- **Server logs:** our hosting provider keeps standard technical logs (such as IP
  address, browser type and time of request) for security and reliability.

We do not sell your data and we do not show advertising.

## 5. How long we keep it
- Account and synced progress: until you delete your account.
- Payment records: as long as the law requires for tax and accounting [period].
- Server logs: [e.g. up to 30 days] as set by our hosting provider.
- Data saved only in your browser stays until you clear it.

## 6. Your rights
Under the NDPA you can ask to access, correct, export, or delete your data, object
to or restrict some processing, and withdraw consent at any time.

You can do most of this yourself:
- **Export:** Settings > Export backup, or Account > export.
- **Delete:** account menu > Delete account. This permanently removes your account
  and synced progress.

For anything else, email forgecodeacademy.help@gmail.com. We will reply within
[30] days. You can also complain to the **Nigeria Data Protection Commission
(NDPC)**.

## 7. Children
Forge is for learners aged **12 and over**. Learners under 18 must have a parent
or guardian's permission to create an account. If you are a parent and believe
your child has given us data without permission, contact us and we will delete
it.

## 8. Security
We use HTTPS everywhere, hashed passwords, per-user database access rules, and
security headers. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong, unique
password.

## 9. Changes
If we change this policy we will update the date above and, for important
changes, tell signed-in users in the app or by email.
